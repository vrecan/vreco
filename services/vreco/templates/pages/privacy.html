Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Introduction

Vreco LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates our mobile games (the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our Games.

By using our Games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use certain features of our Games (such as leaderboards, accounts, or social features), you may provide or we may collect information including, but not limited to: account identifiers, usernames or display names, and other information you choose to share. Basic game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

Account and Gameplay Data

For Games that offer accounts, leaderboards, or similar features, we collect and process gameplay-related data through third-party service providers. This data may include, without limitation:

  • Account identifiers (e.g., user ID)
  • Username or display name
  • Game statistics (scores, achievements, progress, play time)
  • Leaderboard data (rankings, scores, and associated identifiers)
  • Aggregate and anonymized analytics (usage patterns, feature adoption, performance metrics)
  • Other gameplay-related data necessary to operate current or future features

We may collect additional categories of data as we introduce new features (such as social features, cloud save, achievements, or multiplayer functionality). Such data will be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for updates.

Information Collected Automatically

Our third-party advertising partner may collect certain information automatically, as described in the Third-Party Advertising section below.

Third-Party Data Processors

We use Talo (trytalo.com) to process, store, and manage account data, game statistics, leaderboards, and related information. Talo acts on our behalf and is contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify. Talo stores data on cloud servers located in Germany.

Data processed by Talo may include the categories described in "Account and Gameplay Data" above. For more information about how Talo collects and uses data, please review their Privacy Policy.

We may use additional processors from time to time as we add or change services. We select processors that implement appropriate security measures and comply with applicable data protection requirements. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Advertising

Our Games use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location (country, region)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • App usage data (session duration, app version)

For more information about how Google collects and uses data, please review:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • Google AdMob & Ads Privacy

Ad Personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

In-App Purchases

Some of our Games may offer optional in-app purchases (such as removing advertisements). Purchase transactions are processed entirely by the platform's app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). We do not collect or store any payment information.

Data Storage

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. For Games with accounts, leaderboards, or online features, we store the data described in "Account and Gameplay Data" on servers operated by our third-party processors. We retain this data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices and Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, depending on your location. To request access to your data, request correction, or request deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us at the email address below. We will respond to legitimate requests within a reasonable timeframe as required by applicable law.

You can stop collection of certain data by uninstalling our Games or by not using account-based or online features. Note that some data may be retained as necessary for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our Games are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through our Games, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
